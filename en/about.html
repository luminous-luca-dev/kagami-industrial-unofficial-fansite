HAYATO KAGAMI

Those who cannot be serious about play shall never do great work.

The Foundation

Kagami Industrial was founded by my father. Industrial robots, raw materials, heavy machinery — true to the name "Industrial," my father faced "steel" head-on at the forefront of Japanese craftsmanship. Growing up watching his back, this company became not merely a business to me, but the pride of our entire family.

An Unintended Turning Point

The turning point came during a corporate event. The die-cast miniature cars we created as novelties for dealers and shareholders attracted a level of passionate acclaim far beyond our imagination. Attendees were thoroughly captivated by the precision craftsmanship inherited from our heavy industry background, leading to an overwhelming demand for mass production.

As a result, we officially launched the Hobby Division, and as the resident hobby enthusiast, I was assigned to lead it. That was the moment "what I love" truly became my profession.

Three Distinct Faces

As CEO — I place immense value on integrity and courtesy. I strive to engage with everyone respectfully and maintain well-rounded, thoughtful judgment.

As a Musician — I hold an uncompromising passion for vocals and sound design. Once I take hold of the microphone, the gentle voice gives way to intense, soul-stirring shouts and undeniable acoustic power.

As a Boy — When standing before exciting toys, games, massive mecha, or formidable rivals, I instantly return to the state of an energetic schoolboy. Relentlessly pursuing "coolness" with unwavering loyalty and hot-blooded spirit — that boyhood heart remains my ultimate asset.

Embracing New Challenges

Our PR partnership with ANYCOLOR Inc. started when I personally auditioned for Nijisanji. Having the President & CEO debut as a Virtual Liver to promote our own products was an unprecedented PR strategy. Guided by the core principle of "entertaining our listeners," we will continue pursuing rich entertainment experiences.

Giants of steel, miniature cars in your palm. All passions are equal.
Holding fast to this belief, I look forward to walking forward alongside all our shareholders.

President & CEO
Hayato Kagami
