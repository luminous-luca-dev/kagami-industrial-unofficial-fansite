Management Policy

Corporate Management Policy

To all shareholders: Your warm support is Kagami Industrial's most treasured asset.

Medium-to-Long-Term Vision

Kagami Industrial pursues dual-pillar management balancing heavy manufacturing with virtual entertainment. Stable cashflows from foundational industrial contracts empower aggressive innovation and creative ventures in consumer entertainment.

Growth Strategy

  1. Expanding civil infrastructure projects throughout Asian markets via our Indonesia branch
  2. Global scaling of our TCG products and interactive digital gaming platforms
  3. Commercialization of SMC Lab's next-gen energy research
  4. Solidifying first-mover leadership across virtual metaverse entertainment

Shareholder Return Policy

Enhancing shareholder returns is among our highest priorities. Beyond sound dividends, we enrich shareholder relationships through exclusive physical rewards and immersive shareholder meeting experiences.
