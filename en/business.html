Heavy Industry Division

HEAVY INDUSTRY DIVISION

Our founding business encompasses the research, development, and manufacture of industrial robotics and heavy construction machinery. Based on the sturdy technical foundation laid by the former president, we have expanded into civil infrastructure projects like tunnel excavation, actively contributing to global initiatives via our Indonesia branch.

Key Highlights & Track Record
  • • Infrastructure projects operated by Indonesia branch
  • • Large-scale tunnel and subterranean excavation initiatives
  • • Development of next-generation autonomous industrial robots

HOBBY & ENTERTAINMENT DIVISION

Established following the inauguration of CEO Hayato Kagami. By applying ultra-precision metallurgical machining techniques perfected in heavy manufacturing, we deliver top-tier collector toys, models, and card games worldwide.

Featured Products & Lines
