About Us

A Boy's Dream, Forged with Grown-Up Craft.

Kagami Industrial is a one-of-a-kind enterprise that fuses engineering prowess cultivated in industrial robotics and heavy machinery with an insatiable passion for toys and entertainment.

Upon the solid industrial foundation built by the founding generation, second-generation CEO Hayato Kagami raised a bold new vision — steering the company with the resolve to turn what he loves into his life's work.

Business Domains

Heavy Industry Division

Hobby & Entertainment Division
