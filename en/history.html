The Path of Kagami Industrial

19XX
Founding of Kagami Industrial
Founded by the father of Hayato Kagami. Commencing manufacturing of industrial robots, materials, and heavy machinery, establishing the backbone for Japan's heavy industry advancement.
20XX
Die-Cast Car Novelty Sparks Phenomenon
Novelty die-cast miniature cars presented to dealers and shareholders ignited unprecedented fan passion. This directly spurred the launch of the Hobby Division, spearheaded by Hayato Kagami himself due to his lifelong love of hobbies.
20XX
Hayato Kagami Appointed President & CEO
Hayato Kagami takes the helm as President & CEO, concurrently establishing the Hobby & Entertainment Division. Promulgating the motto "A boy's dream, forged with grown-up craft," we expanded into trading cards and assembleable model kits.
2019.07
PR Partnership with ANYCOLOR Inc.
To actively promote company goods, the CEO personally auditioned for Nijisanji. Signing a strategic PR partnership with ANYCOLOR Inc., he debuted as a Virtual Liver, astonishing listeners with outstanding vocals and original music on stream 1.
2019.08
Inception of SMC Laboratory
Formed technical advisory agreements with scientific researcher Fuyuki Hakase and arcane specialist Rena Yorumi. Established the tripartite "SMC Lab" to engineer next-generation energy and motive power.
2020.07
Next-Generation 3D Technology Showcase
Executed a landmark live showcase utilizing state-of-the-art 3D avatar rendering, drawing a record-breaking 130,000+ concurrent shareholder viewers and broad industry acclaim.
2020.11
Headquarters Immortalized in TCG "WIXOSS"
Through an official collaboration with WIXOSS, Kagami Industrial's HQ was printed as a playable field card—famously receiving card status before the CEO himself.
20XX
Founding of Kagami Technical High School
As part of corporate CSR initiatives, we established Kagami Technical High School. Dedicated support to youth athletics, notably the baseball club, has produced countless legendary Koshien tournament performances.
PRESENT
Forging the Future
Expanding international infrastructure tunneling via our Indonesia office, official Duel Masters streaming activities, group diversification, and SMC Lab breakthroughs—Kagami Industrial's challenge continues without pause.
