The Duelist

Trading card game players strongly preferred. We welcome Duel Masters fans (those who can talk Bolbalzak Ex), Yu-Gi-Oh! enthusiasts (Dark-attribute Dragon devotees), and MTG players (Grixis enthusiasts). Experience with Duel Masters Plays is a major plus.

Vocal Passion

Individuals with an overwhelming passion for singing. Active participation in corporate karaoke contests is encouraged, with potential duet opportunities alongside the CEO. High acoustic power is an advantage.

Boyhood Heart

Anyone who reverts to a child when entering a toy store. Geno Breaker, Ex-S Gundam, Tieren, Mazinkaiser—if "cool!" makes your eyes sparkle, you belong with us. Model kit assembly skills are highly valued.
