Youth Development & Dream Support

As part of our commitment to social contribution, Kagami Industrial established and operates Kagami Technical High School. Rooted in the spirit of engineering and wholesome athletic development, we nurture the next generation of creators and sports leaders.

Eco Recycling & "SDGs"

The entire Kagami Group participates in plastic resource recycling. During an on-site facility audit, President Kagami personally crushed 20 PET bottles in 30 seconds barehanded.

Observing fellow members of ROF-MAO hailed his strength ("Pure gorilla..."), leading to our sustainability campaign name: "Smash & Destroy Gorilla solution" (SDGs).

Our Commitment

Building a Sustainable & Joyful Society

Kagami Industrial continuously resolves social challenges through its business endeavors. Cultivating educational platforms, eco-conscious construction, and harmonious communal bonds form the foundation of our work. We firmly believe a world where everyone can engage seriously in play is a truly sustainable society.
